The force density method helps to generate quickly shapes for prestressed and (inverted) handing structures. Due to the nature of the method, it can be used in early design stages for an instant exploration of multiple feasible solutions. It can generate solutions of discrete networks, through linear systems of equations, that are in an exact state of equilibrium, without needing iterations. The method, originally developed for cable nets, is very common in the design practice of tensioned membrane roofs. By introducing loads, it also allows the form finding of synclastic structures, highly suitable for efficient shell structures.  The method is independent from material properties. The materialization comes after the form is found. The method allows also to convert the loads at nodes to internal forces in the net.
